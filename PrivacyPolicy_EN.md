# Privacy Policy — Wooden Fish Zen (ForWall)

**Effective date: October 31, 2026**
**Last updated: October 31, 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application "Wooden Fish Zen (ForWall)" (the "App"), developed and operated by [Developer / Legal Entity Name] ("we", "us", or "our"). We understand the importance of your personal information and are committed to protecting it. **In short: the App requires no account, does not collect your identity information, and does not upload your usage data to our servers.**

Please read this Policy carefully before using the App. By starting to use the App, you confirm that you have read and agree to this Policy.

---

## 1. What Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information We Do **Not** Collect

The App requires no registration or sign-in. Accordingly, we do **not** collect:

- Your name, gender, date of birth, or government ID number;
- Your phone number, email address, or mailing address;
- Your location;
- Your contacts, messages, or call logs;
- Photos or videos in your photo library;
- Microphone or camera data;
- Device identifiers (IDFA / IDFV) or clipboard contents;
- Your browsing history, search history, or behavioral profile.

The App contains **no** third-party advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or push-notification SDK, and does not perform personalized recommendations or user profiling.

### 1.2 Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following data is generated and stored locally on your device (iOS `UserDefaults` / App Group shared storage). **It is never uploaded to us or to any third party's servers:**

| Data | Content | Purpose | Storage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tap records | Today's tap count, total tap count, last tap time, currently selected skin | Display statistics, restore last state | Local storage |
| Background settings | Currently selected tapping background, its offset and scale values | Restore your personalized background | Local storage |
| Preferences | Sound on/off, haptics on/off, daily reminder on/off | Remember your settings | Local storage |
| Purchase status | Identifiers of purchased wallpaper products, cached membership status | Determine whether content is unlocked | Local storage |

This data is used solely to provide App functionality. You can delete all of it at once by uninstalling the App.

### 1.3 System Permissions

The App requests the following system permission only when needed for a specific feature, and only after you take the relevant action:

| Permission | Trigger | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Photos — "Add Only" | When you tap "Save to Photos" | **Used only to write an image; we do not read anything in your photo library.** Declining does not affect other features; only saving is unavailable. |

The App does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, Bluetooth, health data, or notifications. Audio playback uses the system "ambient" category and plays built-in sound effects only; no recording takes place.

### 1.4 In-App Purchase Information

Single-wallpaper purchases and membership subscriptions are processed through Apple's App Store in-app purchase system:

- **Payment is handled entirely by Apple.** We never receive, collect, or store your card number, billing address, Apple ID password, or other payment details.
- We only receive from the system the result of whether a product was purchased successfully or whether a subscription is currently active, in order to unlock the corresponding content.
- To confirm whether a subscription remains valid, the App uses Apple's receipt-verification capability to verify the receipt with Apple's servers. This process is performed by Apple, and the handling of related information is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- Purchase records are also kept in your device's local storage so that content can be recognized as unlocked while offline.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the local information described above only to:

1. Display your tap statistics and your current skin and background;
2. Remember your sound, haptics, and other preference settings;
3. Determine whether a wallpaper or wooden-fish skin is already unlocked, so you are not charged twice;
4. Write a wallpaper image to your photo library when you tap Save;
5. Maintain basic operation and troubleshooting (limited to runtime state on your device; nothing is uploaded).

We do **not** use this information for advertising, profiling, credit assessment, automated decision-making, or any purpose unrelated to those listed above.

---

## 3. Where and How Long Information Is Stored

- **Location:** All data is stored on your own iOS device. It is not stored on our servers, so we do not maintain any centralized store of your personal information.
- **Retention:** Data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the App. After uninstalling, local data is removed by the operating system together with the App (iOS may retain App data in device backups, which are controlled by you and Apple).
- **How to delete:** Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Wooden Fish Zen → Delete App; or overwrite existing records in the App by selecting a different background or skin.

- **Sharing:** We do not share your personal information with any third party. The only third-party involvement is Apple's in-app purchase and receipt-verification service (see Section 1.4).
- **Transfer:** The App has no account system, and we hold no transferable set of personal information. In the event of a merger or acquisition, the data practices described in this Policy would not change materially, and we would notify you prominently.
- **Public disclosure:** We do not publicly disclose any of your information.
- **Legal exceptions:** We may provide information that we are legally required to provide only where explicitly required by law or in response to a lawful request by a judicial or administrative authority acting under due process. Because we do not hold your personal information, such situations are in practice extremely rare.

## 6. Your Rights

Because the App has no account and collects no information that identifies you, your rights are exercised directly on your device:

- **Access and correction:** You can view tap statistics and your current skin and background in the App, and change them by selecting different options.
- **Deletion:** Uninstalling the App deletes all local data; you can also overwrite existing settings within the App.
- **Withdrawing consent:** You may disable the App's "Add Photos" permission at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. Other features remain unaffected.
- **Managing subscriptions:** View, change, or cancel subscriptions under Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
- **Complaints and inquiries:** Contact us using the details at the end of this Policy. We will respond within 15 business days.

## 7. Security

We take the following measures to protect your information:

1. The App maintains no user database, structurally avoiding the risk of centralized data breaches;
2. Bundled image assets are stored in encrypted form, reducing the risk of direct asset extraction;
3. All user data resides within the iOS app sandbox, protected by system permission mechanisms;
4. In-app purchases and subscription verification rely on Apple's security mechanisms.

Please understand that no technical measure can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device passcode secure and prevent unauthorized use of your device.
